Getting there: every place is matched to its nearest usable airport. Vienna has year-round nonstops to Istanbul (IST and Sabiha Gökçen), Ankara, İzmir and Antalya, and summer-season nonstops to Bodrum and Dalaman; everywhere else is one stop through Istanbul. Flight times and fares are typical 2026 ranges for a return booked a few weeks out, not quotes — check Turkish Airlines, AJet, Pegasus and SunExpress. Ground distances are road-estimated from the airport and assume an average 62 km/h, which is optimistic on eastern mountain roads and pessimistic on the new motorways.
On the ground: intercity buses reach almost everything and are cheap; a hire car is worth it in Cappadocia, Lycia, the eastern Black Sea yaylas and the southeast. Museum entry is priced separately for foreign visitors at state sites and the Museum Pass Türkiye pays for itself in about three sites. Prices shift with the lira — treat every figure here as an order of magnitude, not a quote.
Photos: Wikimedia Commons contributors under their respective free licences; photographer and licence are shown on each place. Season scores assume normal years: interior Anatolia and the southeast pass 40 °C in July and August, eastern passes and yayla roads are snowbound roughly November to April, and the eastern Black Sea is green because it rains. Check locally before a long drive.
